Drive Mad: Extreme Stunt Driving Challenge

Push your driving skills to the limit with insane jumps, flips, and stunts in this physics-based monster truck madness!

How to Play Drive Mad

Controls:

  • Accelerate: Up Arrow or W
  • Brake/Reverse: Down Arrow or S
  • Steer Left/Right: Left/Right Arrows or A/D
  • Reset Vehicle: R (when stuck)

Game Objectives:

  • Complete crazy stunt courses without flipping over
  • Land massive jumps perfectly
  • Navigate impossible obstacles
  • Reach the finish line in one piece

Game Features

  • Realistic Physics: Challenging vehicle handling that tests your skills
  • Multiple Vehicles: Choose between different monster trucks and stunt cars
  • Dangerous Tracks: Insane courses with jumps, loops, and obstacles
  • Stunt System: Perform flips and tricks for style points
  • Progression: Unlock new vehicles and tracks as you improve

About the Game

Drive Mad is an intense physics-based driving game where players must navigate increasingly difficult stunt courses. The game combines realistic vehicle physics with impossible tracks to create a challenging and addictive experience.

Gameplay

Each level presents a new driving challenge with ramps, loops, and obstacles. The key is to maintain control of your vehicle while performing the necessary stunts to complete the course.

Vehicles

  • Monster Trucks: Powerful but harder to control
  • Stunt Cars: More precise handling for technical courses
  • Special Vehicles: Unlock unique vehicles as you progress

Playing Drive Mad

You can play Drive Mad directly in your browser with no downloads required. The game is perfect for players who enjoy challenging physics-based driving games.

Is Drive Mad multiplayer?

Currently the game is single-player only, focusing on mastering the challenging courses.

How many levels are there?

The game features multiple increasingly difficult tracks, with new ones added in updates.
