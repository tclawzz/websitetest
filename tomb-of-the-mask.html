Tomb of the Mask: The Addictive Maze Climber

Navigate through mysterious labyrinths, collect glowing coins, and avoid deadly traps in this fast-paced arcade adventure!

How to Play Tomb of the Mask

Controls:

  • Move: Swipe in any direction (mobile) or use arrow keys/WASD (PC)
  • Auto-run: Your character keeps moving until hitting walls
  • Change direction: Tap/swipe in new direction

Game Objectives:

  • Climb as high as possible through the maze
  • Collect yellow coins to increase your score
  • Avoid red spikes and other deadly traps
  • Find and use power-ups to help your climb

Game Features

  • Retro Pixel Art Style: Colorful, nostalgic visuals
  • Fast-Paced Gameplay: Quick reflexes required
  • Procedural Levels: Endless maze variations
  • Power-Ups: Temporary abilities to help your climb
  • Leaderboards: Compete for high scores globally
  • Unlockable Masks: Different characters with unique abilities

About the Game

Tomb of the Mask is an addictive arcade game developed by Happymagenta that combines elements of classic maze games with endless climbing mechanics. The game features simple controls but challenging gameplay that tests your reflexes and planning skills.

Gameplay

Players control a character wearing a mysterious mask that automatically runs in one direction. The goal is to navigate through procedurally generated maze-like levels, collecting coins while avoiding deadly traps. The further you climb, the more difficult the challenges become.

Power-Ups

  • Shield: Temporary protection from spikes
  • Magnet: Attracts nearby coins
  • Slow Motion: Gives you more reaction time
  • Double Points: Increases coin value

Playing Tomb of the Mask

You can play Tomb of the Mask directly in your browser with no downloads required. The game is perfect for quick play sessions but hard to put down!

Is Tomb of the Mask endless?

While the mazes are procedurally generated for endless variety, the game does have an ending point that only the most skilled players can reach.

How do you get new masks?

New masks are unlocked by achieving certain milestones or collecting enough coins. Each mask offers unique visual styles and sometimes special abilities.
